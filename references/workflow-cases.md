# Observable workflow cases

Use these cases when improving the skill or reviewing a specific artifact. They are not a score or a mandatory extra round.

- Course video: acquire metadata, original cover, timed subtitles and useful formats; segment long input and inspect complete frame states before teaching from them.
- Field entry: identify actual prerequisite gaps, build a complete learning unit and provide independent practice with reasoned feedback.
- Paper dependency: trace the inherited method to the agreed baseline, preserve original figures where useful and separate it from the focal contribution.
- Mathematical explanation: preserve assumptions and numerical values, explain operations, test a representative calculation and check a meaningful limit.
- Controlled animation: compute state once, synchronize plot and explanation, and provide pause, manual exploration, reset and a static/print alternative.
- Missing tools or access: take a permitted alternative while preserving the learning goal; identify the unmet format or evidence requirement.
- Continuation or local revision: reuse accepted sources, notation and attempts, repairing affected material without regenerating the whole tutorial.

The supplied decay lesson has numerical, isolated DOM and standalone-build tests. These establish behavior and packaging within their scope. A real learner attempt or visual inspection must be recorded separately.
