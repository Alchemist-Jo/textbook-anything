# Evidence behind the field-entry method

These sources inform the teaching decisions in [learning from scratch](learning-from-scratch.md). They support selected mechanisms of learning; they do not validate this skill, its budget tiers, or a claim that a generated tutorial produces mastery. Descriptions below stay within the inspected text or abstract.

| Source | Finding or guidance used here | Design implication and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Carnegie Mellon, Assessing Prior Knowledge](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/teach/priorknowledge.html) | Prior knowledge can be assessed through direct work and indirect self-reports; the result can change the teaching allocation. | Use a small relevant probe or existing work, and distinguish observed ability from assumed background. A self-report alone does not establish mastery. |
| [National Academies, How People Learn II, Chapter 5](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/24783/chapter/7) | Knowledge organization and reasoning interact with prior knowledge; the chapter also discusses limits of self-explanation when relevant knowledge is weak. | Establish the needed concepts before asking a newcomer to explain an unfamiliar mechanism independently. A universal “discover it yourself” rule is inappropriate. |
| [IES, Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/practiceguide/1) | The guide recommends combining graphics and verbal explanation, connecting concrete and abstract representations, alternating worked examples and practice, retrieval, and explanatory questions. Evidence strength varies across recommendations. | Use a linked example, visual, and exercise sequence, then revisit important ideas. Adapt to the topic; do not turn the recommendations into a rigid chapter template. |
| [Chi et al., 1989, Self-Explanations](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1) | The study examines how students explain worked examples while learning to solve problems. | Prompt for the reason behind a meaningful step rather than asking only for the final answer. Do not treat a prompt as evidence that the learner supplied a correct explanation. |
| [Atkinson, Renkl, and Merrill, 2003](https://experts.azregents.edu/en/publications/transitioning-from-studying-examples-to-solving-problems-effects-/) | The inspected abstract reports two experiments combining faded worked steps with prompts to identify underlying principles; it distinguishes near and far transfer. | Reduce support while keeping attention on the principle. A successful nearby exercise does not by itself establish broad transfer. |
| [Karpicke and Blunt, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1199327) | The study reports benefits of retrieval practice in the science-learning tasks it investigated, compared with the studied elaborative condition. | Include a task that requires recall or reconstruction, not just another reading. This does not establish that all concept maps or all forms of elaboration are inferior. |

For use in a particular field, verify its technical content separately. Research on learning provides guidance for sequencing and practice; it cannot establish the correctness of an unfamiliar theorem or a paper's empirical claim. Review allocations and progress labels are editorial tools. A future study of learner outcomes would need real learners, an appropriate comparison, and assessments aligned with the intended transfer.
